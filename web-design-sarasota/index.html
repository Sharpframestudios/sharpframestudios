01Who This Is For

Sharp Frame Studios is an independent studio in Sarasota, Florida. Strategy, website design, development, photography, film and social media are handled by one person, start to finish. There are no handoffs, no account managers and no stock imagery.

That suits a local business that wants a website and a social presence built around what it actually sells, by someone who knows the area. If you also serve Lakewood Ranch, Siesta Key, Bradenton or Venice, say so on the site. A customer outside the city limits will often assume the answer is no unless the page tells them otherwise.

02What the Studio Does

Website design and development. Custom sites designed around your business, audience and goals, responsive on desktop and mobile, with a contact route that is easy to find.

Photography and film. Original images and video made for your website and campaigns, so the site does not rely on generic stock.

SEO foundations. Titles, headings, page structure, internal links, speed and accurate business information are handled during the build rather than bolted on afterward. Nobody can honestly promise a position on Google, and this studio does not.

03How a Project Runs

Strategy. A review of the business and its audience, the goals for the site, page structure, creative direction and a content plan.

Design and development. Custom layout, typography and interactions, built to work on every screen size.

Content and launch. Photography and video where the project needs them, copy, SEO foundations, testing and launch.

The pace is usually set by content, not design: current photos, accurate service descriptions and a clear list of what you sell. That is why content planning happens in the first stage.

06Common Questions

How long does a website take?

Most projects go from strategy to launch within several weeks. Page count and how ready your content is set the pace more than anything else.

Do I need a full rebuild or just a refresh?

Sometimes a site is structurally fine and needs new content. Sometimes it works against you. Our list of twelve signs a business has outgrown its site is a quick way to tell.

Will the site rank on Google?

No one can ethically promise that. A well-built site makes the business easier for people and search engines to understand, which improves the odds. For a practical overview, see our local search guide for Sarasota.

Is a website worth it if my customers find me on social media?

The website is the one asset you own outright. Social platforms rent you an audience on terms they can change, so the two work best together.
