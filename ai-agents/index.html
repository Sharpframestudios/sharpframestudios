( 06 ) GOOD QUESTIONS

Can I keep my current phone number?

Usually, yes. Your number stays where it is, and calls reach the agent through call forwarding or an integration with your phone system. Which route fits is checked during setup.

Can the agent transfer calls to my team?

It can, when your plan and setup include it. Transfers follow rules we define together: who takes which calls, at what hours, and what happens when nobody picks up.

Can it book appointments?

Yes, when it is connected to your calendar or booking workflow. What it can offer and confirm depends on how that workflow is configured for your business.

How does setup work?

We collect your business information, map the call workflows you need, connect the agent to your phone line and tools, and test real scenarios with you before it takes calls.

What happens when the agent needs help?

Uncertain cases follow a handoff or fallback defined in advance: a transfer to your team, a message taken for a call back, or a clear next step for the caller.

How is call usage billed?

Usage is defined by your final plan. Each plan includes a defined minute allowance, and additional usage is billed separately. The exact terms are agreed before your agent goes live.
