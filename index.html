What does Sharp Frame Studios specialize in?

Our primary focus is custom website design and development. We also provide photography, videography, visual direction, SEO foundations, and social media support when a project requires a complete digital presence.

How much does a custom website cost?

Pricing depends on the size, complexity, functionality, content requirements, and creative direction of the project. Every website is scoped individually before development begins.

How long does a website take to build?

Most projects move from strategy to launch within several weeks, depending on the size of the website, client feedback, content availability, and required functionality.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every website is designed responsively across desktop, tablet, and mobile, with layouts and interactions optimized for each screen size.

Can you create the photography and video for my website?

Yes. One of Sharp Frame Studios' key advantages is that website design and original visual content can be produced together. Photography, video, drone footage, and branded content can all be created specifically for the final website.
